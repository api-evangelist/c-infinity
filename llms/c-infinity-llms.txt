# C-Infinity

> C-Infinity builds foundational AI for mechanical design and manufacturing — AI that reasons about geometry, motion, spatial constraints, and production logic. Its platform, AutoAssembler, connects to existing CAD and PLM environments to automate assembly process planning, accelerate engineering change order (ECO) reviews, and generate production-ready assembly work instructions.

Founded 2023, headquartered in Mountain View, California. Raised $16M in April 2026 led by Canaan Partners, with Inventus Capital, Bee Partners, and Radius Capital participating.
